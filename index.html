Curiosity Looped Uploader

A private tool used by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels.

Privacy policy

This app only accesses the YouTube accounts of its owner, using Google sign-in, to upload videos, set thumbnails and schedule publishing. It does not collect, share or sell data about any other person. Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.
